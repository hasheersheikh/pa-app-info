harmes-agent

App information & privacy policy · personal, single-user application

What this app is

A private, personal-use assistant operated by Hashir Sheikh. It connects to the owner's own Google Calendar so the assistant can read the owner's schedule and create or update events — helping plan their day and work routine. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar only — scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar
  • Nothing else. The app never requests Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Photos, or any other Google service.

How your data is handled

  • Calendar data is processed locally on the owner's own computer, inside Docker containers.
  • No data is sold, shared with, or sent to any third party. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's machine and never leave it except when talking directly to Google's API.
  • Events themselves remain in Google Calendar; the app stores no separate copy beyond local operation logs.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access, which immediately invalidates the app's tokens.
